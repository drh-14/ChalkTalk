## Why

The Followup Discussions heading is visually oversized relative to the rest of the question detail. Reducing its size makes the discussion section easier to scan.

## What Changes

- Reduce only the Followup Discussions heading's effective responsive font size by 20%.
- Increase the space between the question body and the students' answer by about 10%.
- Keep Pin and Merge as duplicate at their original button size and place them below the post body, before the answer panels.
- Keep the compose prompt, followup bodies, and other headings at their current sizes.

## Capabilities

No capability requirements change. This is a visual styling adjustment, so this change opts out of a spec delta.

## Impact

The question-detail layout and CSS change. There are no API, database, or dependency changes.
